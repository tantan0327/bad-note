A practice notebook for badminton players.

Note: the app interface is in Japanese only.

Your coach tells you something. You notice something during a match. You remember it that day, and by the next practice it is gone. BadNote solves exactly two problems: forgetting, and losing motivation.

■ Work down from where you want to be

Vision, then the tournament you are aiming at, then three focus issues for the week, then your practice log. You do not pile up faults and grind them down; you start from the player you want to become. The weekly focus holds three issues at most, and adding a fourth asks which one it replaces.

■ Resurface forgotten issues, once a week

The most common failure is an issue you wrote down and never came back to. Once a week, BadNote surfaces the issues you have not touched, with the reason attached: "four weeks, never made it into your focus" or "six practices since it left your focus."

It only suggests. It never rewrites your priorities on its own. Three choices settle it on the spot, and you can adjust the thresholds in settings.

■ Every field is optional

Log only the minutes, only how it felt, or a single line about something you noticed. Any of those saves. Sorting things out can wait until later.

How it felt has three levels: done, closer, next. When the same issue comes up as "done" three times in a row, the app offers to raise its mastery level once. You decide.

■ At-home training

Fifty swings. Ten minutes of core work. A short daily checklist for what you do at home. Minutes are adjustable per day, and you can go back and tick yesterday. This time counts toward your total.

■ Accumulated time

Your lifetime practice total only goes up. Cross one hour, five, ten, and the app marks the moment. The ladder runs all the way to ten thousand hours.

■ It does not push you

Deliberately missing: streaks, unread badges, a running count of open issues, and the words "failed" or "missed." Notifications are capped at one per day. In the two weeks before a tournament, the app stops digging up old issues so you can concentrate.

■ Your data

No network access at all. Everything is stored on your device, and there is no account to create. You can export all of it from settings at any time.

Works for both singles and doubles. In doubles, issues are still written as your own.
